# DaemonState licensing

DaemonState 0.3.0 and later are source-available under the
[Sustainable Use License 1.0](../LICENSE), SPDX identifier `SUL-1.0`.
The license text—not this summary—controls.

## What the license permits

- Personal and noncommercial use.
- A company using and modifying its own self-hosted installation for its
  internal business.
- Studying, auditing, and modifying the source for those permitted uses.
- Sharing original or modified copies free of charge for noncommercial
  purposes, provided the license and copyright notices remain included.

Anyone distributing a modified copy must also add a prominent notice saying
that it was modified.

## What it does not permit

- Selling copies or paid distributions of DaemonState.
- White-labeling DaemonState as a commercial product.
- Operating DaemonState as a paid hosted or managed service for other people.
- Selling access to DaemonState's functionality, or making DaemonState a
  substantial source of value in a paid customer offering.

A different commercial use requires a separate written license from every
copyright holder whose work is included.

SUL 1.0 does not categorically prohibit every non-visible backend use inside a
distinct commercial product. Its line is between a company's own internal
business use and providing or distributing DaemonState to others. If a planned
use sits near that boundary, obtain legal advice or a written commercial
license rather than relying on this summary.

## Why this is called source-available

The public can inspect and use the source under the permissions above, but the
license restricts commercial use. The Open Source Definition requires
[commercial use to be allowed](https://opensource.org/faq#can-open-source-software-be-used-for-commercial-purposes),
so describing this version as “open source” would be inaccurate.

## Earlier versions

This change is not retroactive. Versions through 0.2.0 were distributed under
the MIT License, and anyone who validly received those versions keeps the
rights the MIT License granted. The exact final MIT-licensed source boundary is
commit [`45b7a6e653a1762bf91b99fae4c7adf3dafc55ce`](https://github.com/Darshan174/DaemonState/commit/45b7a6e653a1762bf91b99fae4c7adf3dafc55ce).
Later source distributed with the current `LICENSE` file uses SUL 1.0.

## Contributions

There is currently no contributor license agreement. Outside code and
documentation contributions are therefore paused; bug reports and product
feedback remain welcome. Before accepting outside copyrighted work, the
project owner should adopt a lawyer-reviewed agreement that grants the rights
needed for centralized commercial exceptions and future relicensing. A later
agreement would not automatically cover contributions already accepted.

Third-party components retain their own licenses; see
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt).
